[title: "ChartRenderer Class"] ← Back to index

Renders notes, receptors, and chart elements

[heading: "Description"]

Handles all visual rendering of the chart during gameplay, including notes, receptors, hold/roll bodies, mines, beat lines, and speed/background change indicators. Supports both falling and rising scroll directions with configurable speed modifiers. Manages sprite pooling and z-ordering for efficient rendering performance.
