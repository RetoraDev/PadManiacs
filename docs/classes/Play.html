Main gameplay state

[heading: "Description"]

The Play state runs a single song from start to finish. It clones the chart, loads the audio and backgrounds, spawns the player renderer and advances the rhythm gameplay loop producing judgement counts, combo, accuracy and a final score. When the song ends it collates the results and hands them to the Results state.

[heading: "Key Features"] [list] * Full playthrough flow from audio setup through judgement and results * HUD with score, combo, lifebar, accuracy, visualizers and character closeups * Background
